# Persona & Future-State Journey, Module 2

- **Scenario:** StreamLine Spotlight (B2C)

## Persona
- **Role:** For the premium customers / cinema enthusiasts that value high quality content
- **Goal:** Make it easy to discover exceptional quality films based on expert curation and recommendations  without leaving the Streamline platform
- **Friction:** I open StreamLine wanting to relax and watch something, but there are so many options that I spend a long time browsing, comparing and second-guessing instead of actually watching.

## Current workaround (the status quo to beat)
- **External tools:** IMDb ratings and reviews
Reddit movie recommendation threads
Google searches ("best movies on StreamLine")
YouTube reviews and trailers
Friends/family group chats for recommendations
- **The process:** Open StreamLine and browse recommendations.
Scroll through multiple titles without feeling confident enough to choose.
Leave StreamLine to check IMDb, Reddit, Google, or reviews.
Return to StreamLine and compare several options.
Either start a movie after a long delay or abandon the session altogether.
- **Core frustration:** The process breaks down when the user sees several "possible" options but lacks confidence that any of them will be worth their limited evening viewing time. Rather than making a decision, the user leaves StreamLine to seek validation elsewhere.
- **The evidence:** "I spend more time deciding what to watch than actually watching."

## Future-state journey map
1. Discover: Browse Spotlight rail → instantly see curated choices → reduces choice overload
2. Trust: Read curator rationale → gain confidence in recommendations → reduces recommendation skepticism
3. Decide: Select from trusted shortlist → choose faster → reduces decision paralysis
4. Enjoy & Return: Watch and follow curated paths → discover consistently great content → reduces churn risk

## Competitive advantages
Trusted recommendations without leaving StreamLine.
Confident choice in minutes, not endless scrolling.
One seamless journey from discovery to playback.
