## Goal & Audience
- **Goal:** Engagement, The persona is using the product, but not as much as they could and as a result there is a risk of them churning.
- **Target audience:** Existing subscribers with a history of browsing but lower-than-expected viewing activity, indicating potential disengagement due to content choice overload.

## Launch Tier
- **M, Targeted**, Medium-complexity launch with strategic engagement impact. It is an established product and the target persona subscribes to it. What we want is to ensure they feel greater value and engage more often.

## Success Metrics
- **Metrics:** Goal: 
Increase engagement among existing subscribers experiencing content choice overload by helping them discover relevant content faster and start watching more often.

Primary succes metric

- Median same-session Time-to-Play

Secondary succes metric

- % of users exposed to Spotlight who watch at least 10 minutes of content selected from Spotlight

Leading Indicator

- Spotlight CTR

Business Outcome Metric

- Average weekly viewing sessions per user
- **Bad signal to watch for:** Session Abandonment Rate
- **Likely post-launch decision:** Expected decision: If Time-to-Play improves and Abandonment remains stable or decreases, proceed with full rollout and test personalised Spotlight recommendations in a follow-on experiment.
