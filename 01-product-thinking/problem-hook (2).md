# Problem Hook & Value Proposition, Module 1

- **Scenario:** StreamLine Spotlight (B2C)

## Strategic crisis
Customer Impact
- Users continue to experience too much content to browse through and difficulty finding something to watch and may give up as a result.
- Some users may as a result choose to leave to other platforms

Business Impact
- Churn will continue to increase
- Engagement remains  declines.
- Additional spend on customer acquisition becomes necessary to remain at current size
- Investment in own content may receive lower returns due to churn or lack of discovery

Competitive Impact
- Competitive streaming services may strengthen their positioning.
- StreamLine becomes known primarily for being the frontrunner, but with nothing good to see

Strategic Risk
- The company's historical advantage of having the largest catalog becomes less valuable.
- Additional content investment and acquisition fails to generate sufficient ROI 
- StreamLine risks becoming known as lagging behind competitors

## Moment of misery
I open StreamLine wanting to relax and watch something, but there are so many options that I spend a long time browsing, comparing and second-guessing instead of actually watching. I also use other platforms to help guid eme.

## Problem hook
Users currently spend significant time browsing before starting playback.  If we do not fix they may end up cancelling their subscription.

## Value proposition
For For the premium customers / cinema enthusiasts that value high quality content, we will Make it easy to discover exceptional quality films based on expert curation and recommendations  without leaving the Streamline platform because Or else they will cancel their subscription and it will be hard to acquire new customers.

## Cold-read self-review
The consequences are clear and dramatic.
